About WD-NCL

The whitedragon-dev Non-Commercial License (WD-NCL) is a carefully crafted license template that balances the freedom to share and collaborate with the protection of the creator's commercial rights.

It was designed to address a common frustration among developers: the desire to share their work openly while preventing others from commercially exploiting it without permission.

Philosophy

The WD-NCL is built on three core philosophical principles:

  • Freedom to Share: Knowledge and software should be freely available for learning, research, personal use, and collaboration. The license grants broad permissions for non-commercial use.
  • Protection from Exploitation: Creators should have the right to decide who profits from their work. The license prevents third parties from selling or commercially exploiting the software without permission.
  • Zero Liability: Software is provided as-is, with no warranties and absolutely no responsibility for the copyright holder. Users assume all risks.
In One Sentence: "Free for everyone to use non-commercially – but only the copyright holder can make money from it."

Why Use WD-NCL?

For Project Owners

  • Protect Your Work: Prevent others from selling or commercially exploiting your software without your permission.
  • Maintain Control: You retain full commercial rights while still sharing freely with the community.
  • Zero Liability: You are not responsible for how others use your software.
  • Attribution: You receive credit whenever your work is used or shared.

For Users and Contributors

  • Free to Use: Use the software for personal, academic, and non-profit purposes at no cost.
  • Free to Modify: Modify and improve the software for your own needs.
  • Contributions Welcome: Your contributions are welcomed and credited when merged.
  • Clear Terms: The license clearly defines what is and isn't allowed.

Who Should Use WD-NCL?

The WD-NCL is ideal for:

  • Individual Developers who want to share their work but keep commercial rights.
  • Academic Researchers who want to share code for reproducible research.
  • Non-Profit Organizations that share tools and resources.
  • Open-Source Projects that want to prevent commercial exploitation.
  • Hobbyists and Personal Projects that want to share freely.
Note: This license is not OSI-approved because it restricts commercial use by third parties, which violates the Open Source Definition. This is intentional.
