About WD-SAL

The whitedragon-dev Source-Available License (WD-SAL) is a carefully crafted license template that balances the freedom to share source code with strong protections for the creator.

It was designed for developers who want to go beyond a simple non-commercial license by adding explicit patent protection and prohibiting sublicensing by third parties.

Philosophy

The WD-SAL is built on four core philosophical principles:

  • Freedom to Share: Source code should be freely available for learning, research, personal use, and collaboration.
  • Protection from Exploitation: Creators should have the right to decide who profits from their work and how it is licensed.
  • Patent Protection: Users should not fear patent claims from the copyright holder, and those who sue lose their rights.
  • Zero Liability: Software is provided as-is, with no warranties and absolutely no responsibility for the copyright holder.
In One Sentence: "Free for everyone to use non-commercially – with patent protection, no sublicensing, and zero liability."

Why Use WD-SAL?

For Project Owners

  • Protect Your Work: Prevent others from selling or commercially exploiting your software.
  • Patent Protection: Users get a patent license, but lose it if they sue you.
  • No Sublicensing: Others cannot relicense your code under different terms.
  • Zero Liability: You are not responsible for how others use your software.

For Users and Contributors

  • Free to Use: Use the software for personal, academic, and non-profit purposes.
  • Free to Modify: Modify and improve the software for your own needs.
  • Patent Peace: You receive an explicit patent license from the copyright holder.
  • Clear Terms: The license clearly defines what is and isn't allowed.
