What Is WD-SAL?

The whitedragon-dev Source-Available License (WD-SAL) is a license template designed for developers who want to share their source code while maintaining strict control over commercial use, patent rights, and sublicensing.

Key Principles:
  • The copyright holder retains full commercial rights.
  • No third party may use the software commercially without explicit written permission.
  • Patent protection – explicit grant with termination on patent litigation.
  • No sublicensing – third parties cannot relicense your code.
  • Zero liability – strongest possible disclaimer.

Who Is This License For?

This license is ideal for:

  • Open-source projects that want patent protection and prevent relicensing
  • Academic and research software
  • Personal projects and hobbyist code
  • Non-profit organizations sharing tools
  • Developers who want to maintain commercial control while sharing freely

Quick Start

To apply the WD-SAL v1.0 to your project:

  1. Copy the full license text from the License Text page.
  2. Replace [Year] with the current year.
  3. Replace [Copyright Holder Name] with your name or organization.
  4. Replace [Your Jurisdiction] and [Your City, Your Country] with your legal location.
  5. Create a file named LICENSE in your project's root directory and paste the text.
